How Local Ingestion Works

1
Text Extraction

PyMuPDF extracts page text for PDFs, python-docx parses paragraphs & tables, and openpyxl/csv parses tabular data.

2
Semantic Chunking

Documents are split respecting paragraph and sentence boundaries. For CSV/XLSX, 1 complete row = 1 logical chunk.

3
Local Embeddings

Sentence Transformers (all-MiniLM-L6-v2) generates 384-dimensional dense vectors entirely on your local machine.

4
ChromaDB Storage & Isolation

Vectors are saved with strict user_id metadata to guarantee absolute user isolation during retrieval.
